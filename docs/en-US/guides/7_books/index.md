# Books & Resources

Amateur radio is a lifelong learning pursuit. Whether you are studying for your first license, building your first antenna, or diving into advanced digital modes, quality reference material makes all the difference. This section compiles recommended books, online resources, and a comprehensive glossary of amateur radio terms.

## What You'll Find Here

- **[Recommended Books](./7_1_recommended_books.md)** -- Essential references, study guides, and technical books for all experience levels
- **[Online Resources](./7_2_online_resources.md)** -- Websites, forums, software, mobile apps, and YouTube channels for amateur radio
- **[Glossary](./7_3_glossary.md)** -- An alphabetical reference of 50+ amateur radio terms and their definitions

## How to Use This Section

If you are **new to amateur radio**, start with the beginner study guides in the Recommended Books section, then explore the online resources for forums and communities where you can ask questions.

If you are an **experienced operator** looking to expand your knowledge, the technical references (ARRL Handbook, Antenna Book, etc.) and specialized online resources will be most valuable.

The **Glossary** is designed as a quick reference. If you encounter an unfamiliar term anywhere in this wiki or in amateur radio in general, check there first.
